A few years back, while surfing the net, I stumbled across Palestinian DJ Sama' Abdulhadi and was completely amazed by her set. 
In the years following I have thoroughly enjoyed her live performances and original productions. 

She is a hero to me.

Three years ago, out of frustration with every music player available, I started building my own. Many iterations, many failures, and more than a few moments of giving up later, SAMA exists. 
I think you will be very satisfied with it.

SAMA short for Spectral Audio Morphing Application is a installable desktop audio media player.
A windows certification is in process and Google Play store is next.
